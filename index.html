01 / 33 years of Nigerian farming records

Nigeria Yield Gap Explorer

Is Nigeria growing more food because farming got better, or because farmers cleared more land?

Finding128% of the growth came from clearing more land. The amount harvested from each hectare actually fell. Food grown per person peaked in 2006 and is 10.5% lower now.

What I didMeasured how much of the growth came from land and how much from yield, two separate ways, to be sure the methods agreed. They did. Then tested whether more fertilizer would close the gap, using farming records from 221 countries.

Why it mattersFertilizer helps less than you would expect in countries that already use very little, which is exactly where Nigeria sits. So the obvious fix is not the fix. Maize was the one crop that improved through better farming, so it can be done here.

02 / 68 markets across 14 states

Price Early Warning System

Which everyday foods are most likely to jump in price over the next few months?

Finding50% of the foods it warns about do go on to jump sharply in price. Normally only 29% of months end that way. It tells you what to watch, not what will happen.

What I didMy first version did not work. Instead of adjusting it until the result looked good, I tested each part on its own and found one part was pointing the wrong way. Nigerian food prices usually settle back down after a rise, and I had not allowed for that. I rebuilt it so it only ever learns from what already happened, never from anything it could not have known at the time.

Why it mattersThe strongest warning sign turned out to be the naira to dollar rate, worked out from the price records themselves. Most price trackers in Nigeria do not look at the exchange rate at all.

03 / Trade and harvest data, worldwide

Import Disruption Risk

If food imports were cut off, which countries would run short first?

Finding35% A 35% drop in imports would leave Japan short of 5.3 million tonnes of maize. Move the slider to any level and it works out any country and any crop.

What I didCombined how much a country leans on imports, how unsteady those imports are, and how unsteady its own harvests are, into one score. I chose how much each of those three counts rather than calculating it, and I say so on the page instead of presenting it as something measured.

How it gets usedChange the import shock assumption and see straight away which countries and which crops become most exposed. A manager answers their own question in half a minute instead of waiting on someone.

04 / Daily market monitoring

Market Volatility Risk

Is this market calm or shaky right now, and has that just changed?

Finding Two separate methods judge the market and both are shown side by side. When two reasonable methods disagree, that disagreement is worth knowing about.

What I didIt watches how sharply prices are moving, sets the market at low, medium or high risk, and raises an alert the moment that changes.

Why it mattersIt can be plugged into other software, so another system checks the risk level without a person sitting and watching a screen. This one is not about agriculture, which is the point. The method travels.
