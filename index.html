Agriculture · FAOSTAT & World Bank

Nigeria Crop Growth and Yield Analysis

Has production growth come from harvesting more area, improving yields, or both?

Approach

I analyzed eight crops, separated changes in a weighted production index into area and yield components, and compared Nigerian yields with selected African peers.

Finding

Patterns varied by crop. Maize yield rose about 84% while harvested area rose about 14%, comparing 1990–1992 with 2021–2023. The weighted index showed a positive area component and a negative yield component.

These results describe observed patterns, not their causes. The index is not the same as a change in total tonnes produced.
